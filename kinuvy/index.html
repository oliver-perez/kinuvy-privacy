Kutsi by Kinuvy · For dogs

Kutsi — your dog’s day, care and training.

A calm training coach and a daily companion for your dog, on iPhone and iPad.

Short lessons where Kutsi, a drawn Akita puppy, shows the moves step by step. The day’s plan and diary, walks, and a health tracker for vaccines, medication, weight and vet visits, with a report you can share with your vet.

Free for every dog

  • Today: the day’s plan and diary, with meals, walks, notes and photos.
  • Walks with the route and the distance, also on the Lock Screen while you walk.
  • Care: vaccines, deworming and check-ups on one plan, medication reminders, a weight chart and a symptoms log.
  • A report for the vet: a clear PDF of what you logged, ready to share or print.
  • Three training programs in full (Foundations, Puppy start and Cooperative care), every short read, and one lesson to try in each other program.
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, several dogs, and iCloud sync between your iPhone and iPad.

Kutsi Pro

Kutsi Pro opens the rest of the training coach, for every dog in your home, and can be shared with your family. Choose a yearly or a monthly plan, each with a 7-day free trial if your Apple Account is eligible, or pay once and keep Kutsi Pro for good. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; see the Terms of Use.

What Kutsi doesn’t do

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your dogs’ records stay on your devices and in your iCloud; read the Privacy Policy. Training is reward-based only. Kutsi keeps track and reminds you; it doesn’t diagnose. Your vet does.

Built to work with VoiceOver throughout and with the largest text sizes. In English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German and Italian.
