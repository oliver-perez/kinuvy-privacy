Kutsi Pro

How do I restore Kutsi Pro?

Open Pack › Kutsi Pro › Restore purchases (also in Pack › Settings › Your plan and purchases). Use a device signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If someone in your family bought Kutsi Pro and shares purchases through Family Sharing, it turns on by itself and shows “Shared by your family”.

How do I cancel or change a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Pack › Kutsi Pro › Manage subscription, or on your iPhone or iPad go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kutsi. There you can cancel or switch between the yearly and monthly plans. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, or of the free trial, to avoid the next charge; Kutsi Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Deleting the app or your data does not cancel a subscription. If you buy the lifetime purchase while you have a subscription, cancel the subscription yourself as above. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. More in the Terms of Use.

What is free, and what needs Kutsi Pro?

Free for every dog: Today and the diary, walks, all of Care, the vet report, iCloud sync, the widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, the Foundations, Puppy start and Cooperative care programs, every short read and one lesson to try in each other program. Kutsi Pro opens the rest of the training coach. A lesson your dog has finished stays open.

Your records

How do iCloud sync and a second device work?

Kutsi keeps your dogs’ records in your own private iCloud, so they appear on every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. There is no account with Kutsi. On each device:

  1. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account.
  2. In Settings › your name › iCloud, check that Kutsi is on in the list of apps that use iCloud.
  3. Install Kutsi and open it. On a new device it looks for your dogs in iCloud first; this can take a moment.

Pack › Settings › iCloud should say “On”. Changes usually arrive within minutes with an internet connection; Low Power Mode can delay them. If the same dog was added on two devices before they synced, Kutsi offers to merge the two profiles, keeping everything logged on both. Each device sends its own reminders: turn them off on a device that should stay quiet. Kutsi works without iCloud too; your records then stay on that device.

How do I delete all data?

Open Pack › Settings › Delete all data. It deletes every dog and everything logged, and with iCloud on, it removes them from every device signed in to that iCloud account. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel Kutsi Pro. To remove just one dog, open Pack › Edit profile › Remove. More in the Privacy Policy.

Reminders and the vet report

Reminders are not arriving

  • Check that notifications are allowed for Kutsi: Settings › Notifications › Kutsi, or Pack › Settings › Notification settings.
  • In Pack › Settings › Reminders, check that the kind you expect is on (Medication and care, Potty timer, Daily training nudge).
  • Open the vaccine, task or medication and check that it has a reminder.
  • A Focus, such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb, or Scheduled Summary can hold notifications back.
  • Reminders are scheduled on each device, so a device with notifications off will not show them.

Reminders help you remember; for a medication your dog cannot miss, keep another way to remember too.

How do I share the vet report?

Open Care › Report for the vet (also in Pack), choose the period, and tap Share the report. Send the PDF by Mail, Messages or AirDrop, save it to Files, or print it. The report is made on your device and goes only where you send it. It is a summary of what you logged, not a medical record.

More

Which languages does Kutsi speak?

English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German and Italian. Kutsi follows your device’s language. To use another language for Kutsi only, go to Settings › Apps › Kutsi › Language. Units (kilograms and kilometres, or pounds and miles) are in Pack › Settings › Units.

A walk has no route

Kutsi draws a route only with location allowed while using the app. In Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Kutsi, choose “While Using the App”, and turn on Precise Location for an accurate route. Without location, a walk runs as a timer.

I used the earlier version of the app

Kutsi 3.0 was rebuilt for dogs, and records from the earlier version cannot be shown in it. The app says so once, when you update. If you have questions about them, write to us before you delete the app.
