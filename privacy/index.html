Kutsi by Kinuvy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what Kutsi, the app for dogs and their owners on iPhone and iPad, keeps, where it keeps it, and the little that leaves your device. It applies to Kutsi 3.0 and later. Kutsi was earlier published under the name Kinuvy; this policy replaces the one written for that earlier app.

In short

  • There is no account with Kutsi, and Kutsi has no servers.
  • Your dogs’ records are kept on your devices and, when iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud database. We cannot read them.
  • Kutsi Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store. RevenueCat, which handles purchases for us, receives an anonymous id and your purchase history, and nothing about your dogs.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Kutsi keeps records and reminds you. It does not diagnose: your vet does.

1. Who we are

Kutsi is developed and published by Kinuvy (“Kinuvy”, “we”, “us”). We are responsible for the app and for the little personal data described in this policy. For any question about privacy, or to use any of your rights, write to:

kinuvy.support@gmail.com

2. What Kutsi keeps

Kutsi keeps what you enter in it:

  • Your dogs’ profiles: name, breed, birthday, sex, size, whether the dog is neutered, the illustrated portrait you choose or a photo, your goals, your vet’s name and phone number, the microchip number, notes and any other record you add.
  • The diary: meals, walks (their time, distance and route), pee and poop, water, notes and photos, weight, symptoms, medication doses given, care done and lessons practised.
  • The care plan: vaccines, deworming, check-ups and other tasks, and medications with the dose and schedule you typed in.
  • Training progress: the lessons done and how they went.
  • Your settings (units, appearance, sounds, haptics and reminder times), kept on each device.

Kutsi does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or home address, and works without any of them.

3. Where it is kept

  • On your device, in the app’s own storage, protected by iOS and iPadOS.
  • In your own iCloud, when your device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Kutsi. Your records are then also kept in your private iCloud database (Apple’s CloudKit), so they appear on your other iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple keeps that database under the iCloud terms and Apple’s privacy policy. It is private to your Apple Account: Kinuvy cannot see, read or download it.
  • Kutsi works fully without iCloud. Without it, your records stay on that one device, and they sync once iCloud is on.
  • A walk’s route is stored with the walk, as part of the diary, and syncs with it.
  • The widgets, the walk’s Live Activity, and Siri and Shortcuts read what they show from Kutsi’s storage on the same device.

Kutsi has no servers of its own, so we hold no copy of your dogs’ records.

4. What leaves your device

Only the following.

Apple

  • iCloud sync, as described above, when iCloud is on.
  • Purchases: Kutsi Pro is bought and billed through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Maps: the map shown under a walk’s route comes from Apple Maps, which loads the map of that area from Apple.
  • Siri: what you say to Siri is handled by Apple. Kutsi’s Siri and Shortcuts actions run on your device.
  • If you have chosen, in your device’s settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Apple’s handling of this data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

RevenueCat, for purchases

RevenueCat, Inc. processes in-app purchases for Kutsi, so that Kutsi knows whether Kutsi Pro is on and can restore it. It receives:

  • an anonymous, random app user id made by the app (not your name, e-mail address or Apple Account);
  • your purchase history in Kutsi: which plan, when it started, renewals, trials and the App Store transaction ids;
  • technical details such as the App Store country and the app version, and the network (IP) address that any connection over the internet carries.

It receives nothing about your dogs. Kutsi turns off RevenueCat’s collection of device identifiers and never sends RevenueCat a name, an e-mail address or any other detail about you. RevenueCat acts as our service provider; see RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What you choose to share

  • The vet report is a PDF made on your device from what you logged. It goes only where you send it with the share sheet (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or a printer). Once you have sent it, the person or service you sent it to handles it.
  • An e-mail you write to us (see section 7).

Nothing else leaves your device.

5. Permissions and why Kutsi asks

  • Location, only while using the app. Kutsi uses your location only while a walk with a route is running, to draw the route and measure the distance. During that walk it keeps recording while the phone is locked or Kutsi is in the background; the system’s location indicator and a Live Activity show while it runs, and recording stops when the walk ends. Kutsi never asks for “Always” access and never uses your location outside a walk. If you allowed only approximate location, Kutsi asks for precise location for that walk only. If you decline, the walk runs as a timer.
  • Notifications. Reminders for care (vaccines, deworming, check-ups and other tasks), medication doses, a puppy’s potty breaks and a daily training nudge, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder two days before it ends. They are local notifications, scheduled on your device; each device with Kutsi sends its own. Kutsi works fully without them.
  • Photos. You choose photos with the system photo picker. Kutsi sees only the photos you pick and asks for no access to your photo library and no camera permission.

You can change any permission at any time in your device’s settings.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Kutsi shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not use your data to build a profile of you. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

7. When you write to us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and what you write, with any attachment. We use them only to answer you, we add you to no list, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed to help you, or sooner if you ask. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), under Google’s privacy policy.

8. Children

Kutsi is made for dog owners and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, for example by e-mail, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay until you delete them. You can delete them at any time, without asking us:

  • One dog: in Pack, choose Edit profile, then Remove. This removes that dog’s profile, diary, care plan, medication and training progress.
  • Everything: in Pack, open Settings and choose Delete all data. This cannot be undone.
  • With iCloud on, both remove the records from every device signed in to that iCloud account, and from your iCloud.
  • You can also delete Kutsi’s data from iCloud in your device’s settings, under your name › iCloud, where your iCloud storage is managed.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device; the copy in your iCloud stays until you delete it as above.

Deleting your data does not cancel Kutsi Pro: a subscription is cancelled in your Apple Account settings (see the Terms of Use).

RevenueCat keeps the purchase history as long as it is needed to provide and restore your purchases and to meet legal and accounting duties, as its privacy policy describes. If you want that record deleted, write to us: as the id is anonymous, we will ask you for something that lets us find it, such as the order ID on your App Store receipt. Kutsi Pro would then need to be restored from the App Store.

10. Your rights

Your dogs’ records are on your devices and in your iCloud, so you can see, correct and delete them in the app at any time, and share a summary of them with the vet report.

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws such as the GDPR in the European Union and the United Kingdom, Brazil’s LGPD and Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP, under which these are known as ARCO rights) give you rights over your personal data: to access it, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy of it, and withdraw consent you gave. For the data we or RevenueCat hold for us (your e-mails to us and the purchase history), write to kinuvy.support@gmail.com and we will answer within the time the law that applies to you sets. You can also complain to the data-protection authority where you live.

Where the GDPR applies, we use that data because it is needed to give you what you asked for: Kutsi Pro after you buy it, and an answer when you write to us. Apple, RevenueCat and Google may process it in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards.

11. Security

Your records are protected by your device’s own security and, in iCloud, by Apple, which encrypts iCloud data in transit and on its servers. Keep your device locked with a passcode and your Apple Account secure: anyone who can open them can see your records.

12. Health information

Kutsi keeps track and reminds you; it does not diagnose, treat or calculate medication doses. You type the dose your vet prescribed. The vet report is a summary of what you logged, not a medical record. For your dog’s health, and always in an emergency, contact your vet or the nearest emergency clinic.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change concerns what leaves your device, we will say so here before the version of Kutsi that brings it is released.
