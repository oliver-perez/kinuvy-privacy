2. The app and what is free

Kutsi is free to download. Without any purchase, for every dog:

  • Today’s plan and the diary, walks with their route, and the widgets, Siri and Shortcuts;
  • all of Care: vaccines, deworming, check-ups, vet visits, medication reminders, weight and symptoms;
  • the vet report and iCloud sync;
  • in Train: the Foundations, Puppy start and Cooperative care programs in full, every short read, one lesson to try in each other program, and the training tools.

Kutsi Pro opens the rest of the training coach: the other lessons of the remaining programs and their checks. Nothing about care, health or safety is ever locked, and a lesson your dog has finished stays open, whatever happens to your plan.

3. Kutsi Pro: plans and payment

Kutsi Pro is offered as:

  • a yearly auto-renewable subscription;
  • a monthly auto-renewable subscription;
  • a lifetime one-time purchase.

All three open the same features, for every dog in your home. The price of each plan is shown in the app, in your currency, before you buy, and may differ between countries. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.

4. Automatic renewal and cancellation

  • The yearly and monthly subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, for the same period, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • If the price of a subscription changes, Apple tells you beforehand and, where its rules or the law require it, asks for your agreement.
  • To cancel or change a subscription, go to your Apple Account settings: on iPhone or iPad, Settings › your name › Subscriptions. In Kutsi, Pack › Kutsi Pro › Manage subscription takes you there.
  • When you cancel, Kutsi Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed. Deleting the app or deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.

5. The free trial

  • The yearly and monthly plans include a 7-day free trial for Apple Accounts that are eligible for it. The app says whether your account is eligible before you start.
  • At the end of the trial, the subscription starts automatically and is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The app shows the date the trial ends and the date of the first charge.
  • If you allow notifications, Kutsi reminds you two days before the trial ends.
  • Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription or the lifetime purchase.

6. The lifetime purchase

The lifetime purchase is a single payment. It does not renew and is never charged again, and it keeps Kutsi Pro on for your Apple Account. Buying it does not cancel a subscription you already have: cancel that one yourself in your Apple Account settings, as above.

7. Family Sharing and restoring purchases

All three plans can be shared through Apple’s Family Sharing, when it is set up and purchase sharing is on for your family. To restore Kutsi Pro on a new or another device signed in with the same Apple Account, open Pack › Kutsi Pro › Restore purchases.

9. Training content

Kutsi’s lessons use reward-based methods only: no force, no punishment and no aversive tools. They are general guidance for owners, not professional advice, and they do not replace a qualified trainer or behaviour professional who can see your dog. Go at your dog’s pace and stop a session if your dog seems in pain, afraid or stressed. For aggression, strong fear or anxiety, or a sudden change in behaviour, talk to your vet and a qualified behaviour professional. You are responsible for your dog’s safety and for the safety of the people and animals around it while you train or walk.

10. Health information

Kutsi keeps records and reminds you. It does not diagnose, treat or calculate medication doses, and it does not replace a veterinarian. Vaccine and deworming schedules are shown as typical schedules, to be confirmed with your vet; you type the dose your vet prescribed. The vet report is a summary of what you logged, not a medical record. Reminders depend on your device and its notification settings, so do not rely on them alone for a medication your dog cannot miss. If you think your dog is unwell, and always in an emergency, contact your vet or the nearest emergency clinic.
